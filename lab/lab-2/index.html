Lucky Me! Noodles

This is my grandmother's secret recipe that has been passed down for generations.

Ingredients:

  • 1 Lucky Me! Instant Noodles
  • 1 large egg
  • Black pepper and salt

Instructions:

  1. Boil water, and cook noodles until soft
  2. In a bowl, whisk together the egg
  3. Open seasoning packet, and pour it into the noodles
  4. Pour the whisked eggs, and wait it for it to cook
  5. Pull away from heat, and transfer to a bowl.

Simple but delicious!

Cooking Tips

After years of experimenting here are my tips:

  • taste your food as you cook
  • Keep your knives sharp
  • Read the entire recipe before you start cooking
  • prepare all ingredients before you begin

Remember: cooking is about experimenting and having fun. Don't be afraid to make mistakes - they often lead to the best discoveries!
