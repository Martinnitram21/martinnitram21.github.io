AzaBuild — Construction Estimation System

Laravel 12 + React Quantity takeoff & costing CPM-PERT scheduling Offline Android app

A construction management system I built single-handedly for Azacon Builders and Realty OPC in Davao City, now live and in daily use by the firm. One specification drives material quantities, itemized cost estimates and a CPM-PERT schedule, using coefficients from Max Fajardo's Simplified Construction Estimate (1995) that estimators can check against the book. It adds weather-aware delay tracking, branded PDF reports, an Android app that syncs field updates offline, and AzaBuild Lite, a no-account material calculator for homeowners.

Visit azabuild.cids.io
Live · Commissioned by the Municipality of Sulop

eSkolar+ (LSAS) — Scholarship Administration System

LGU commissioned Laravel + React · MySQL CRS + GGMS integration Solo developer Exhibited at RSTW 2026

A web-based scholarship management system commissioned by the Municipality of Sulop, Davao del Sur, covering the full scholarship lifecycle — application intake, review, and disbursement. Built with Laravel, React and MySQL, it validates beneficiaries against the Civil Registry System, integrates with GGMS for government fund tracking, exports reports, and is hosted on Hostinger. I was its solo developer.

eSkolar+ is part of eSulopGov, the municipality's group of integrated digital systems, which is now live. As the student leader of the eSulopGov program, I managed the other students' systems and spoke with municipal stakeholders, and I exhibited the group at the 2026 Regional Science, Technology, and Innovation Week (RSTW) in Davao del Sur alongside other participating institutions.

Source is private — commissioned by the LGU RSTW certificate Visit sulopcris.info

Klaru — Lab Results, Explained in Your Language

Bisaya · Filipino · English Expo · React Native · Supabase Teleconsult Laravel 13 portal Team of 3 + coach

A health-literacy app for Filipino families. Photograph a lab result and Klaru explains every value in plain language, flags how urgent it is, and keeps a whole family's results, trends and visit notes in one place. Built-in teleconsult lets you request an appointment with a health-center doctor and share your result, and a health-center portal (shown as a working preview on demo data) lets doctors review results and answer requests. Klaru explains values; it never diagnoses.

I built the Android app and the Laravel web portal and landing page. Our team from Davao del Sur State College is presenting it at IT Olympiad Davao on October 16.
